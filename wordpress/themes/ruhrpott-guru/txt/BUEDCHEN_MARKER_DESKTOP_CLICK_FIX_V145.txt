RuhrpottGuru v145 – desktop Büdchen marker click fix

Bug:
Clicking a non-overlapping Büdchen marker on desktop bubbled pointer-down into
the map. The map captured the mouse as a drag gesture and a tiny movement could
change the viewport, causing a Büdchen list reload instead of opening detail.

Fix:
- Non-overlapping markers stop pointer-down and pointer-up propagation.
- Marker click prevents default and opens the existing detail overlay directly.
- The parent map never starts drag/pan for a marker click.
- Overlapping markers remain non-clickable exactly as in v144.
- Normal desktop map dragging, wheel zoom, mobile one-finger page scroll and
  two-finger map gestures are unchanged.
