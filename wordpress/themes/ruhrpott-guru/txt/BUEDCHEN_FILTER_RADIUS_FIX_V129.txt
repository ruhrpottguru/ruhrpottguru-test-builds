RuhrpottGuru v129 – Büdchen filter/radius invariant

Hard invariant:
Location + radius + quality selection create the fixed public result set.
Filters only reduce that set.

Example:
5 km = 12 Büdchen
Kaffee <= 12
Sitzplätze <= 12
Jetzt geöffnet <= 12
Kaffee + Sitzplätze <= 12

No filter can ever increase the result count or pull additional Tier-B candidates
into the current radius result.

Time filters and assortment filters remain independent modes:
choosing Kaffee clears an old "Jetzt geöffnet" filter, and choosing a time filter
clears assortment filters.

Map and image fixes from v128 remain included.
Requires Places Backend 1.2.9 for same-origin map tiles and place_media images.
