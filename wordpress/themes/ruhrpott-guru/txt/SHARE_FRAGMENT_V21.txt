RuhrpottGuru – Share Fragment V21

FIXES
- Replaces V20 long query-string share URLs with compact fragment-only links.
- New format: https://www.ruhrpottguru.de/#rg/<category>/<item>/<lat>/<lng>/<city>
- Fragment data never reaches WordPress, preventing canonical/query-routing redirect loops.
- Removes rg_title and category=events from newly generated share links.
- Native iOS/Android sharing remains the primary share path.
- WhatsApp share text uses readable line breaks (title / date-time / city / link).
- Shared item location is held stable while the deep link resolves; GPS bootstrap cannot overwrite it mid-load.
- Shared result is auto-opened once, then the deep-link fragment is consumed with history.replaceState.
- Existing V20 links are migrated server-side to the V21 fragment format at template_redirect priority 0.
- Existing Event Calendar actions and Wesel event multisource logic are retained.
- The previously removed legacy technical tag remains absent.
