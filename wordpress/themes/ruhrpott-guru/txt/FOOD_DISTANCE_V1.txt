RUHRPOTT GURU – FOOD DISTANCE ORDER V1

Goal:
- Food order is based on fresh Haversine distance from the currently selected GPS coordinate.
- No rounding is allowed before sorting.
- Under 1 km, cards show meters so ordering can be visually checked.
- Radius chain stays 1 -> 2.5 -> 5 -> 10 -> 25 km.
- Food Filter V2 remains reversible and unchanged.

Regression invariant:
If distance(Kaiserhof) < distance(Martinelli) for the current GPS coordinate, Kaiserhof MUST appear before Martinelli.
No venue-name hardcoding is used to force that order.
