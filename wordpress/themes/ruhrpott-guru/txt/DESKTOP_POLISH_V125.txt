RuhrpottGuru v125 – Desktop Polish, Phase 1

Hard rule:
- No mobile CSS or JavaScript logic changed.
- Every visual change in this release is scoped to @media (min-width:1024px).

Desktop changes:
1. Existing bottom navigation becomes a desktop portal navigation below the sticky header.
   It reuses the exact same Footer menu/content; mobile footer stays unchanged.
2. Location, detail and feedback overlays are centered desktop dialogs rather than bottom sheets.
3. Home event/activity rows become four-column desktop grids.
4. Food becomes a four-column grid.
5. Highlights get a magazine mosaic with a larger lead card.
6. Culture becomes a two-column scan list.
7. Category controls, cards and detail typography receive modest desktop spacing/proportion polish.
8. Mobile v124 appearance/behavior remains unchanged.
