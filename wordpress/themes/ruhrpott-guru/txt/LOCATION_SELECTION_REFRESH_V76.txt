RuhrpottGuru V76 — WordPress location selection refresh

- Header location marker and every Home/category location CTA open the same shared location layer.
- Category error-card buttons no longer start GPS directly; they open the layer so GPS or a city can be chosen.
- Manual city selection is persisted defensively in the canonical ruhrpottguru_location_preference_v1 key.
- City/GPS selection dispatches ruhrpott:location-changed and rehydrates the visible WordPress magazine/category modules immediately without a hard reload.
- Previous GPS-denied/error cards are invalidated before rehydration, preventing stale "User denied Geolocation" state after selecting a city.
- Places Backend remains v1.1.5; no DB/import changes.
