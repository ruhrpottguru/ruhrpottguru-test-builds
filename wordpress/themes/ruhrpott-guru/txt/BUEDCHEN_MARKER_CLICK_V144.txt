RuhrpottGuru v144 – clickable Büdchen map markers

- A single, visually unambiguous Büdchen marker is directly clickable/tappable.
- Click/tap opens the existing Büdchen detail overlay immediately.
- Markers closer than 24 px are treated as overlapping.
- Overlapping markers remain visible but are not clickable, preventing the wrong
  Büdchen detail from opening.
- Zooming in separates the markers; as soon as they no longer overlap, they
  automatically become clickable.
- Mobile one-finger page scrolling and two-finger map gestures remain unchanged.
