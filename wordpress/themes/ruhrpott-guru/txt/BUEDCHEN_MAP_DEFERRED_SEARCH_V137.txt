RuhrpottGuru v137 – Mobile map deferred search

Goal:
Keep two-finger map interaction fluid by preventing all Büdchen backend requests while
the fingers are still on the map.

Behavior:
- One finger continues to scroll the page.
- Two fingers pan and pinch-zoom the map.
- During the complete two-finger gesture, map state and tiles can update visually,
  but onViewport does not trigger a Büdchen search.
- Each movement only stores the latest pending viewport.
- Only after the final touch finger is lifted is one backend search started for
  the final visible map area.
- Intermediate pauses while fingers remain on screen can no longer trigger queries.
- Desktop mouse drag / wheel behavior is preserved.
- Reset remains immediate.
- Filters, radius rules, Home banner and footer are unchanged.
