RuhrpottGuru v122 – Feedback Abuse Protection

- Based on v121 Feedback Inbox.
- No external CAPTCHA or third-party anti-spam service.
- Same-site HMAC feedback token, valid for 15 minutes.
- Token must be at least 2 seconds old before submission (basic bot timing check).
- One submission per token (replay protection).
- Hidden honeypot field.
- Request body hard limit: 8 KB.
- Message length: 4–1200 characters.
- More than 3 URLs rejected.
- Exact duplicate from same ephemeral client key rejected for 15 minutes.
- Ephemeral rate limits: 5 submissions / 10 min and 15 / hour per salted client key.
- Global emergency ceiling: 150 accepted attempts / 5 min before temporary 429 responses.
- Raw IP address is not written to Feedback or Analytics. The abuse limiter derives a salted one-way HMAC key from REMOTE_ADDR and stores only the temporary key/counter in WordPress transient storage.
- No Google reCAPTCHA, Cloudflare Turnstile or tracking cookie introduced.
- Existing WordPress Feedback Inbox and 12-month retention remain unchanged.
