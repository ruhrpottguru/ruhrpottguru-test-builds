RUHRPOTT GURU – OFFICIAL WEBSITE ENRICHMENT V1

Purpose
- Enrich gastronomy cards from identity-verified official restaurant websites.
- No Google scraping and no Google Places/Billing dependency.

Priority / safety
1. OSM-linked official website (requires venue name + local context on page).
2. If no OSM website: max. two deterministic .de domain candidates.
   A guessed domain is accepted ONLY with strict venue + city + street/house-number identity match.
3. Crawl at most five useful pages on the verified official site.
4. Extract only factual links/metadata found on that official site.

Extracted fields
- Official website
- Food menu / Speisekarte (prefers a clearly labelled PDF when available)
- Online reservation URL (may be an external booking provider only when linked clearly from the official site)
- Opening hours via Schema.org JSON-LD or common German weekday text

Caching
- Successful enrichment: 7 days server-side
- Negative result: 6 hours server-side
- Session cache in browser avoids repeat requests for already-seen cards

UI
- Existing Food Info & Actions buttons are populated asynchronously.
- No placeholder buttons are shown.
- Official opening hours override weaker OSM opening hours when successfully extracted.
