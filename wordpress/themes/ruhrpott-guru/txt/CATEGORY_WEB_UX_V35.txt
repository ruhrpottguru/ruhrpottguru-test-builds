Ruhrpott Guru V35 – Category Web UX

Base: V34 Filter Radius Safe.

Changes:
- On Events, Highlights and Culture overview cards, the redundant category badge is suppressed just like Food.
- If available, the card displays the more useful subcategory instead.
- Safe source-linked websites are shown on overview cards.
- Detail overlays for Events, Highlights and Culture resolve official web presence from:
  1) source/OSM website,
  2) OSM-linked Wikidata P856,
  3) identity-matched destination.one/eT4 Event or POI record,
  4) strict verified domain fallback for stable POIs only,
  5) official Facebook/Instagram profile as last resort.
- Events keep deep links to the exact event/exhibition page where available.
- Search/review/directory URLs are rejected as official websites.
- No menu, reservation, Food price or Food opening-hours logic is transferred to other categories.
- Scene Intelligence and V34 radius handling are unchanged.
