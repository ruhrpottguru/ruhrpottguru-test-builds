RuhrpottGuru v120 – Privacy / Beta retention

- Based on v119 Beta Readiness.
- Beta feedback is automatically deleted after 12 months.
- Cleanup runs daily via WordPress Cron.
- Feedback continues to store only timestamp, page, category and the submitted message.
- No analytics user identifier is added.
- No exact GPS coordinate is added to analytics.
- Aggregate first-party analytics from v118/v119 remain unchanged.
- Commercial/advertising layer remains dormant.
- No Google Ads or external analytics network is introduced.

Privacy-policy wording should distinguish:
1. server/network processing (where IP addresses may technically be processed/logged by hosting);
2. RuhrpottGuru analytics records (IP is not stored as an analytics attribute);
3. beta feedback (12-month retention).
