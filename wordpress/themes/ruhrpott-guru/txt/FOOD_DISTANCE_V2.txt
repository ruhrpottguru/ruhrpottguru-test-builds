RUHRPOTT GURU – FOOD DISTANCE DISPLAY V2

Internal distance math and sorting remain unchanged.
Only presentation is softened:
- < 1 km: "ca. N m", rounded to nearest 10 m
- >= 1 km: one decimal km

Examples:
0.175 km -> ca. 180 m
0.198 km -> ca. 200 m

This avoids implying GPS/OSM precision that is not physically justified.
