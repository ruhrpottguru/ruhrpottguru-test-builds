RuhrpottGuru – Food Mobile UX V22

BASE
- Built from V21 share-fragment release.
- Event/Wesel, calendar and share deep-link logic intentionally unchanged.

FOOD OVERVIEW
- On the Food category overview only, the redundant "Gastronomie →" badge is replaced.
- Exact price tiers (€ / €€ / €€€) are shown when present.

- Both Food action buttons have a minimum 44 px touch target.

FOOD DETAIL
- Red "X Schließen" control replaced by a top-left "← Zurück" control.
- Back closes the detail overlay and preserves the underlying filtered result list.
- Missing Food price copy changed to:
  "Keine Preisinfos vorhanden (Schau am besten kurz in die Karte vor Ort!)"
- Technical source/trust badges are hidden from the upper action area for Food.
- Food source/trust information is rendered near the bottom after travel information.
- Photo attribution on the hero image remains available via the existing overlay.

REGRESSION INTENT
- Non-Food category-card category badges remain unchanged.
- Non-Food hashtag presentation remains unchanged.
- Non-Food compact Route/Details controls remain unchanged.
- Event calendar, Wesel multisource event loading and V21 fragment sharing are untouched.
