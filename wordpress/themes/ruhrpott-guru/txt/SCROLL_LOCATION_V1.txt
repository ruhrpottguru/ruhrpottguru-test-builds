RUHRPOTT GURU – INFINITE SCROLL + SMART LIVE LOCATION V1
Generated: 2026-08-20

CHANGES
- Manual "Mehr Orte laden" control removed from category feeds.
- IntersectionObserver automatically reveals the next 12 already-loaded cards before the user reaches the feed end.
- No extra provider/API request is generated by infinite scroll itself; it only increases visibleCount over the existing result array.
- After card 12, a semantic "↑ Nach oben" link is inserted. It smooth-scrolls to #ruhrpott-category-feed.
- Last successful GPS coordinate is cached locally for up to 6 hours and is used immediately on reload.
- If browser geolocation permission is already granted, a fresh location is requested quietly in the background instead of showing the blocking GPS state.
- The app starts watchPosition only when the browser explicitly reports permission=granted.
- Background movement does not trigger a data refresh for every GPS callback.
- Accepted live-location updates require >=500 m movement and normally >=3 minutes since the last accepted point; movements >=3 km may update sooner.
- GPS fixes with reported accuracy worse than 500 m are ignored by the live watcher.
- High accuracy is disabled for background refresh/watch to reduce battery use.
- When the document is hidden, watcher callbacks do not update application state.
- Existing explicit "Aktuellen Standort verwenden" action remains available.

MOBILE LIMITATION
A normal website cannot reliably keep GPS tracking active after the browser/tab is suspended or closed by Android/iOS. This implementation tracks while the page is active and resumes from the cached point on reload; browser permission rules remain authoritative.
