RuhrpottGuru v121 – Persistent Feedback Inbox

- Builds on v120.
- Adds a permanent WordPress admin menu "RPG Feedback".
- Feedback can be filtered by: All, New, In Progress, Done.
- Search across message, page and category.
- Actions: mark new, in progress, done, or delete.
- Menu badge shows number of new feedback items.
- Existing feedback table is upgraded safely with status + resolved_at fields.
- The feedback system remains usable after the beta; only the frontend wording/beta badge may later change.
- 12-month automatic deletion from v120 remains active.
- No IP/user identifier is added to feedback records.
