RuhrpottGuru v146 – Büdchen marker opens detail overlay

Root cause:
The marker click itself was working, but BudeMap's onPick callback was explicitly
implemented to scroll the corresponding list card into view. Therefore marker
clicks could never open the Büdchen detail overlay.

Fix:
- BudeMap onPick now calls the exact same setDetail(item) state used by clicks
  on Büdchen list cards.
- Non-overlapping markers open BudeDetail immediately.
- No scrollIntoView / list centering is triggered by marker clicks.
- Overlap protection from v144 remains.
- Desktop map drag, wheel zoom and mobile touch behavior remain unchanged.
