PURPOSE

- Changed fingerprint queues exactly one new resumable pass.
- Browser may be closed; WP-Cron continues queued/running jobs.

JOB CONTRACT
- key: stable migration identity
- fingerprint: semantic parser/resolver/input version
- scope: system | active_places | active_events | trusted custom selector
- processor: trusted idempotent method
- batch_size: 1..50
- conflicts: worker lanes that must be idle before writes start
- cursor: monotonic primary ID, persisted after every processed entity
- state: queued/running/waiting/complete/error with processed/changed/skipped/errors

- job state uses a persistent option and a global transient lock.
- failed entities are recorded; any job with errors is NOT marked complete.
- no automatic destructive rollback or mass reset exists in the framework.
