----------------

- A hit is stored through the existing canonical opening_audit_save_hours() path in place_schedules, place_enrichment and places.
- A miss returns to unknown-after-exhaustive-search; it does not become a new research-incomplete case.
- Background worker remains server-side and continues without an open browser tab.
- Delta baseline, queued count and recovered-hit count are stored separately and shown on the admin page.
