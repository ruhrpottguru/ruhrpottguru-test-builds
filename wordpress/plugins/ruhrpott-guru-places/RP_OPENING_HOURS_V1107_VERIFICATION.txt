RUHRPOTTGURU PLACES BACKEND v1.10.7 — OPENING HOURS AUDIT

PURPOSE
- Full server-side opening-hours audit for all active Food, Highlights, Kultur and Aktiv places.
- Reuse reliable existing hours and persist them canonically.
- For relevant places without hours, resolve identity-bound sources through existing RuhrpottGuru theme resolvers: OSM/source record, Wikidata P856, destination.one/eT4, then official website.
- Crawl official site and likely opening-hours/visit/contact pages only; do not use generic directory/Google scraping as canonical evidence.
- Save found hours to rg_place_schedules, rg_place_enrichment and rg_places.
- Track evidence, source URL, attempts and unresolved places in rg_place_opening_audit.

SAFETY
- Public outdoor landmarks/activities that do not require classical opening hours are marked not-required.
- Closed/restricted places are not promoted as open.
- Unknown remains unknown when no reliable source is found.
- External audit is throttled to max 2 remote places per AJAX batch for shared-hosting safety.
- Restarting the audit clears audit status only; already saved opening hours stay intact.

PARSER
- JSON-LD openingHoursSpecification supported.
- Multiple intervals per weekday retained.
- German/English weekdays supported.
- Day ranges supported.
- Official-site text extraction supports multiple HH:MM-HH:MM intervals.
