RUHRPOTT GURU – PLACES BACKEND 1.10.90
ACTIVITIES SOURCE-COVERAGE WORKER CONTROL – FINAL VERIFICATION
Date: 2026-09-18

GOAL
Prevent the normal 15-minute Places municipality cron and the explicit Activities
53-city Source-Coverage worker from sending different municipalities to Overpass
at the same time, without resetting or restarting the running coverage wave.

VERIFIED ROOT CAUSE IN 1.10.89
- Both workers call claim_next_municipality() and server_sync_municipality().
- The Activities worker checks for an already active municipality before claiming.
- The normal 15-minute cron did not check whether the Activities wave was queued/running.
- Therefore, while an Activities city was already inside server_sync_municipality(),
  the normal cron could claim another due city and start a second Overpass request path.

FIX
- The persisted 1.10.89 coverage hook and state option are intentionally unchanged:
  rg_places_activity_source_coverage_tick_v101089
  rg_places_activity_source_coverage_state_v101089
- New helper activity_source_coverage_owns_refresh_lane_v1090() treats queued/running
  as exclusive ownership of municipality refreshes.
- cron_sync_tick() keeps lightweight non-Overpass maintenance but returns before
  claim_next_municipality() while the coverage wave is queued/running.
- An already-running normal municipality sync is not killed or reset. The existing
  Activities worker waits for its municipality lock and continues afterwards.

PROGRESS / QUEUE SAFETY
- No UPDATE/DELETE is added for places_municipalities.
- No coverage state initialization or restart is added on plugin update.
- started_at/requested_at/completed city timestamps remain untouched.
- No wp_clear_scheduled_hook() is added during upgrade/runtime.
- Retry counters and cooldown logic are unchanged.
- Existing progress such as 3/53 therefore continues from the persisted 1.10.89 state.

UNCHANGED
- OSM query construction and provider order
- claim_next_municipality() and server_sync_municipality()
- Activities resolver/matrix and public visitor feed
- Food, Highlights, Culture
- Events, opening hours, REST contracts and Theme
