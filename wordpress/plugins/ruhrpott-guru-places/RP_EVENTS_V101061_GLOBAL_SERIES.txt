RuhrpottGuru Places Backend 1.10.61

Global event-series read model
- Read-only endpoint: /wp-json/ruhrpottguru/v1/events/{eventId}/related
- Explicit ET4/destination.one parent/series/part-of relations win when present.
- Otherwise only high-confidence title families are linked, gated by city, time distance and organizer/venue/series cues.
- Same external_id occurrences are never presented as sibling events.
- Exact duplicate titles are not turned into series siblings.
- Existing event DB rows, ordering, visibility and media are unchanged.
- Up to 24 future/current sibling events are returned with canonical RuhrpottGuru links.
